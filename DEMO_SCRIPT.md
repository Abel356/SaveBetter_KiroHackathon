# SaveBetter - 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script
## "Bob's Financial Transformation Story"

### 🎯 **Opening Hook (15 seconds)**
*"Meet Bob. 26 years old, living in Toronto, making $65,000 a year. Like 73% of Canadians, Bob has been told to save 20% of his income. That's $1,083 per month. But here's the problem..."*

---

## 📖 **STAR Framework Narrative**

### **SITUATION** (30 seconds)
*"Bob tried the traditional 50/30/20 rule. But with Toronto rent at $2,200, student loans, and life happening, saving $1,083 felt impossible. He'd save for two weeks, then give up feeling like a financial failure. Sound familiar?"*

**[DEMO: Show landing page highlighting the problem]**
- Point to "Beyond the 50/30/20 Rule" headline
- Emphasize "Traditional budgeting rules don't work for everyone"

### **TASK** (20 seconds)
*"Bob needed a financial plan that actually fit his life, not some textbook formula. He needed to see ALL his options - not just what banks wanted to promote. And he needed it to be specifically designed for Canadians like him."*

**[DEMO: Navigate to /demo]**
- Show the onboarding flow
- Emphasize "Canadian-focused" and "personalized"

### **ACTION** (90 seconds) - **THE CORE DEMO**

#### **Step 1: Bob's Input (20 seconds)**
*"So Bob tried SaveBetter. He entered his real information..."*

**[DEMO: Fill out the form quickly]**
- Income: $65,000
- Age: 26
- Province: Ontario
- Complete assessments quickly (show the dual system)

*"Notice our dual assessment - we don't just test financial knowledge, we understand Bob's personality and risk tolerance."*

#### **Step 2: The Magic Happens (25 seconds)**
*"Here's where SaveBetter's Adaptive Engine works its magic..."*

**[DEMO: Show results page]**
- Point to the 12% adaptive rate vs 20% traditional
- Highlight the green money psychology
- Read the personalized reasoning

*"Instead of impossible 20%, SaveBetter recommends 12% - that's $650 per month. Achievable, sustainable, and it builds confidence instead of failure."*

#### **Step 3: Complete Canadian Coverage (25 seconds)**
*"But here's what makes us different - we show Bob ALL his options."*

**[DEMO: Click "View All Banking Options"]**
- Show the 22+ institutions
- Demonstrate filtering by Big Six, Online Banks, Credit Unions
- Point to the comprehensive comparison

*"22 Canadian financial institutions. Not just the 'best' - ALL of them. Bob can make truly informed decisions."*

#### **Step 4: Investment Guidance (20 seconds)**
*"And because Bob's assessment showed he's a 'Balanced Explorer', we guide him to appropriate investments."*

**[DEMO: Click "Explore All Canadian Investments"]**
- Show the TSX stocks and ETFs
- Point to the risk classifications
- Show the market overview

*"20+ Canadian stocks, 10+ ETFs, all with risk assessments tailored to Bob's profile."*

### **RESULT** (45 seconds)

#### **Bob's Transformation (25 seconds)**
*"Six months later, Bob has saved $3,900 - more than he'd ever saved before. Why? Because 12% was achievable. He built the habit, gained confidence, and SaveBetter gradually suggested increasing to 14%, then 16%."*

**[DEMO: Show the adaptive progression concept]**
- Explain how the system grows with users
- Emphasize habit-building over perfection

#### **The Bigger Impact (20 seconds)**
*"Bob isn't alone. 2.3 million young Canadians struggle with traditional financial advice. SaveBetter doesn't just give them a budget - it gives them a financial plan that actually works."*

**[DEMO: Return to landing page showing the comprehensive platform]**

---

## 🎯 **Closing Power Statement (20 seconds)**

*"SaveBetter isn't just another budgeting app. It's a comprehensive financial platform built specifically for Canadians, with adaptive intelligence that meets people where they are, not where financial textbooks think they should be."*

**[DEMO: Quick scroll through key features]**
- Adaptive Engine
- 22+ Canadian banks
- TSX investment data
- Dual assessment system

*"Because your financial plan should fit your life, not the other way around."*

- [ ] Dual assessment system explanation
