## **🚨 POTENTIAL HICCUPS & SOLUTIONS**

### **If Page Loads Slowly:**
- **SAY:** "While this loads, let me explain..."
- **FILL TIME:** With technical details or market opportunity

### **If You Make a Mistake:**
- **STAY CALM:** "Let me show you another feature..."
- **PIVOT:** To a different strength of the platform

### **If Questions Come Up:**
- **ACKNOWLEDGE:** "Great question! Let me show you..."
- **USE IT:** As an opportunity to highlight a feature

## **🎤 CONFIDENT DELIVERY TIPS**

### **Voice & Pace:**
- **SPEAK CLEARLY:** Judges need to understand every word
- **VARY PACE:** Slow for important points, faster for navigation
- **PAUSE:** After key statistics for impact

### **Body Language:**
- **POINT:** At screen elements you're discussing
- **GESTURE:** To emphasize the transformation story
- **SMILE:** When talking about Bob's success

### **Technical Confidence:**
- **KNOW YOUR DATA:** 22 banks, 20+ stocks, 10+ ETFs
- **PRACTICE TRANSITIONS:** Between pages until smooth
- **HAVE BACKUP:** Know how to navigate if something breaks
